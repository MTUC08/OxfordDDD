CoffeeMachine

Workflow: StartMachine
Inputs: WaterLevel
Outputs: ReadyState

Workflow: UpdateWaterLevel
Inputs: WaterLevel AND optional Water
Outputs: WaterLevel (updated) AND ReadyState

Workflow: InsertPod
Inputs: ReadyState AND CoffeePod
Outputs: ReadyState (updated)

Workflow: MakeCoffee
Inputs: CupSize AND FrothLevel
Outputs: BrewState

data CoffeePod = DarkRoast OR MediumRoast OR LightRoast OR Decaffeinated
data CupSize = Small OR Medium OR Large
data BrewState = Brewed OR NotBrewed
data WaterLevel = High OR Medium OR Low
data ReadyState = Ready OR NotEnoughWater OR MissingPod
Data FrothLevel = Light OR Medium OR Heavy
